Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to common questions about courses, tutoring, fees, discounts, scholarships and admissions.

1. Which courses does IITian's MANTRAS offer?

Our listed learning programmes include Classes 9–12, JEE Mains and Advanced preparation, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, B.Tech subject tutoring, Core Java, Spring Boot, programming and software development. Contact us to confirm the availability of your chosen subject.

2. Are online and offline classes available?

Online learning enquiries are welcome. Offline classes and their schedules should be confirmed directly with the institute before enrolment. Availability may differ by course and batch.

3. Can I choose one-to-one tutoring?

Yes, you can enquire about individual tutoring. Share your subject, class, preferred schedule and learning goals so the team can discuss suitable options and availability.

4. Are group classes available?

You can enquire about group learning batches. Batch sizes, timings, course availability and the mode of delivery will be confirmed by the team.

5. What are the course fees?

Course fees depend on the programme, duration, learning format and schedule. Contact us by call or WhatsApp for the current fee structure and applicable offers.

6. Are special online discounts available?

Enquire about current discounts on eligible online courses. The applicable offer, amount and conditions should be confirmed before making a payment.

7. Can students from financially weaker families apply for fee waivers?

Students facing financial difficulties can contact the institute to ask about possible fee-waiver or scholarship support. Assistance depends on eligibility, availability and the applicable terms. A waiver is not guaranteed for every applicant.

8. Will my course be completed on time?

The expected completion schedule should be discussed before enrolment. Actual progress depends on the agreed syllabus, class attendance, practice requirements and other course conditions.

9. Do programming courses include practical projects?

Project-oriented learning can be discussed for relevant technical programmes. Ask about the included modules, practical exercises, project scope and prerequisites before joining.

10. How can I contact the institute?

Call or WhatsApp 7004691842 to enquire about courses, fees, schedules, online offers and scholarship opportunities. You can also use the enquiry form on this website.
